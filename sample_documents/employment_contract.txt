EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Executive Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Vanguard Innovations Corp ("Company") and Jane Doe ("Executive").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Executive is employed as Senior Vice President of Product Development. Executive reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer.

2. COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
a) Base Salary: $220,000 per annum, payable in bi-weekly installments.
b) Performance Bonus: Eligible for an annual discretionary bonus up to 25% of base salary based on performance metrics.
c) Health Insurance & 401(k) match per standard company policy.

3. NON-COMPETE & NON-SOLICITATION
During employment and for twelve (12) months following termination:
a) Executive shall not engage in direct competition with Company within North America.
b) Executive shall not solicit employees or clients of Company.

4. TERMINATION
a) By Company Without Cause: Requires sixty (60) days written notice or pay in lieu of notice.
b) By Company With Cause (fraud, felony conviction, gross negligence): Immediate termination without severance.

GOVERNING LAW: State of New York.
