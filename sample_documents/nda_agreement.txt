NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on August 1, 2026, by and between Apex Tech Solutions Inc. ("Disclosing Party"), and Vertex Dynamics LLC ("Receiving Party").

1. PURPOSE
The Disclosing Party agrees to disclose confidential software architecture blueprints, trade secrets, and financial projections ("Confidential Information") to the Receiving Party solely for evaluating a potential joint venture.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY
The Receiving Party agrees to:
a) Maintain strict confidentiality using no less than a reasonable degree of care.
b) Not disclose, publish, or copy any Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent.
c) Use the Confidential Information exclusively for evaluating the business relationship.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Confidential Information does not include information that:
a) Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement.
b) Was already known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure.

4. TERM & TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain in effect for two (2) years from the effective date. The confidentiality obligations regarding trade secrets shall survive termination indefinitely.

5. GOVERNING LAW & ARBITRATION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any disputes arising hereunder shall be resolved through binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA).

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.
Apex Tech Solutions Inc. (Disclosing Party)
Vertex Dynamics LLC (Receiving Party)
