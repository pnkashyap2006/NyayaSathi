COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made between Horizon Commercial Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Nova Logistics Inc. ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
Landlord leases to Tenant Commercial Unit 402 located at 500 Enterprise Way, Floor 4, containing approximately 5,000 square feet of office space.

2. LEASE TERM & RENT
a) Term: Three (3) years commencing September 1, 2026, and ending August 31, 2029.
b) Monthly Rent: $12,500 due on the first day of each calendar month.
c) Security Deposit: $25,000 payable upon execution of this Lease.

3. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall be responsible for routine interior maintenance and utility payments. Landlord agrees to maintain structural walls, roof, and central HVAC systems.

4. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES
If Tenant fails to pay rent within ten (10) days of the due date, Landlord may assess a late fee of 5% and issue a 14-day notice to cure prior to initiating eviction proceedings.

5. RESTRICTIONS ON ASSIGNMENT
Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet the Premises without the express written consent of Landlord.

SIGNED AND SEALED by Landlord and Tenant.
Horizon Commercial Properties LLC (Landlord)
Nova Logistics Inc. (Tenant)
